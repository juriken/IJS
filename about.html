How IdolJapan.store Operates

  • No Middleman Markups: We do not purchase products in bulk to resell at marked-up prices, nor do we run a warehouse or charge handling commissions.
  • Direct Merchant Redirection: Every product card and Quick View modal contains a direct tracking link to authorized retailer CDJapan (Tokyo, Japan).
  • Legitimate Oricon-Eligible Stock: All items fulfilled through our partner are authentic first-party Japanese pressings distributed by major Japanese record labels (Up-Front Works, King Records, Sony Music Japan, Avex, etc.).
  • Direct Worldwide Courier Dispatch: Shipments are packaged in Tokyo and dispatched directly to your address via DHL Express, FedEx International, Japan Post EMS, or Airmail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I pay IdolJapan.store for my CDs or Blu-rays?

No. IdolJapan.store never handles your payment details, credit card numbers, or shipping address. You checkout directly through CDJapan's checkout gateway.

Are first-press bonus photos and posters included?

Yes. When CDJapan lists a pre-order with external bonus items (外付け特典), such as raw member photos, clearfiles, or B2 promotional posters, those are packaged and shipped directly with your CDJapan package.

How do pre-orders and combined shipping work?

CDJapan allows you to consolidate multiple pre-orders into a single shipment if they release in the same month, significantly reducing your international courier costs. Use our Pre-Order Release Calendar drawer on the catalog page to plan consolidated monthly hauls.
