Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Last updated: October 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and CalOPPA

1. Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

IdolJapan.store does not require user registration, login accounts, password creation, or newsletter signups.

  • We never ask for your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect or store payment cards, banking numbers, or financial identifiers.
  • We do not track visitors across third-party websites or sell personal data to advertisers.

3. Third-Party Services & External Retailers

When you interact with external services or click outbound links, their respective privacy policies apply:

  • CDJapan (Neowing Corporation): When you click "Buy" or "Pre-Order", you are transferred to CDJapan. CDJapan may set standard session cookies to credit the referral to our Affiliate ID and process your purchase securely under their Japanese privacy terms.
  • ExchangeRate API (open.er-api.com): We fetch daily public foreign exchange rates to estimate retail prices in your local currency. No personal information is transmitted during these requests.
