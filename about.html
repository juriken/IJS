About IdolJapan.store

Safe Browsing Guarantee

IdolJapan.store does not process transactions, collect credit card details, store user data, or operate a shopping cart. You will never be asked for payment information on this domain.

What is IdolJapan.store?

Founded in 2019, IdolJapan.store is an independent digital archive and release catalog designed specifically for overseas fans of Japanese female idol music. We index CDs, concert Blu-rays, photobooks, and official merchandise across Hello! Project, AKB48 Group, Sakamichi Series (Nogizaka46, Sakurazaka46, Hinatazaka46), =LOVE, Momoiro Clover Z, and Babymetal.

How Does Ordering Work?

We are not a proxy service (like Buyee or ZenMarket) and we are not a flea market (like Mercari). Instead, we operate as an authorized affiliate directory.

When you click any product link on our site, you are redirected immediately to CDJapan (operated by Neowing Corporation in Tokyo, Japan), a licensed and trusted export retailer serving international customers since 1997.
