Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

IdolJapan.store is a static informational catalog. We respect your privacy and do not maintain user accounts, require registration, or run tracking databases.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment data. All browsing is anonymous.

2. Local Storage

Your browser stores your selected UI preferences (such as Dark Mode theme and the latest daily exchange rate) locally in your device's localStorage. This data never leaves your browser.

3. Outbound Links

When you click a link pointing to CDJapan (cdjapan.co.jp), you are subject to CDJapan's own privacy policy and cookie terms. CDJapan manages their checkout sessions independently.
